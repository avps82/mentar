# Logo candidates — Minator

**Status: PARKED, nothing adopted.** The maintainer explored replacing the 🍕
header mark with a "Minator" (Mentar → Minotaur) reading/teaching figure, across
four rendered rounds (flat two-tone reading bull; crescent silhouette; teaching-
at-the-board; reading-together pair; moon-badge silhouette). None clicked enough
to ship; the strongest — a silhouetted horned figure reading against a full moon
with clouds and stars, per the maintainer's second reference — is preserved as
[`logo_minator_moon.svg`](logo_minator_moon.svg).

If revisited:
- wiring plan: inline SVG replaces the `brand-emoji` span in `_base.html` +
  favicon; keep the night palette FIXED across themes (a moon scene recoloured
  orange loses the idea — real logos stay constant).
- alternative route: commission/source the mark. Brief that captures the
  maintainer's direction so far: *flat, geometric, friendly minotaur (rounded,
  child-safe, no aggression) absorbed in reading an open book or teaching from
  one; 2–3 solid colours, no gradients/outlines; legible at 24 px; SVG with
  each colour a separate fill.* Hand-authored SVG hit its craft ceiling here —
  the concept survived four rounds, the polish is what didn't.
