## Primary Level (Years 1 to 6)

### Year 1 (Primary 1)
- Whole Numbers: Count, read, write, compare, and order numbers up to 100.
- Operations: Visual addition and subtraction within 100; concept of multiplication as equal groups.
- Fractions: Concept of halves and quarters using physical objects and shapes.
- Measurement: Compare and measure length using non-standard units; tell time to the half-hour.
- Geometry: Identify 2D shapes (rectangles, squares, circles, triangles) and complete simple patterns.
- Data: Collect and sort data into basic picture graphs.

### Year 2 (Primary 2)
- Whole Numbers: Numbers up to 1,000; place value (hundreds, tens, ones).
- Operations: Addition and subtraction within 1,000 with regrouping; multiplication and division tables of 2, 3, 4, 5, and 10.
- Fractions: Compare and order unit fractions; add and subtract like fractions within one whole.
- Measurement: Measure length in metres/centimetres; measure mass in kilograms/grams; measure volume in litres; tell time to 5 minutes.
- Geometry: Identify 3D shapes (cubes, cuboids, cones, cylinders); identify quarter turns and half turns.
- Data: Read and interpret picture graphs with scales.

### Year 3 (Primary 3)
- Whole Numbers: Numbers up to 10,000; mental calculation strategies for addition and subtraction.
- Operations: Multi-digit multiplication and division; solving 2-step word problems using bar models.
- Fractions: Find equivalent fractions; express fractions in their simplest form; compare fractions with different denominators.
- Measurement: Read digital and analogue time; calculate durations; add/subtract money; measure perimeter and area of rectilinear shapes.
- Geometry: Identify right angles, acute angles, and obtuse angles; identify perpendicular and parallel lines.
- Data: Read, interpret, and construct bar graphs with scales.

### Year 4 (Primary 4)
- Whole Numbers: Numbers up to 100,000; find factors, multiples, prime factors, and common multiples.
- Operations: Multi-digit multiplication (up to 4 digits by 1 digit); multi-step word problems.
- Fractions: Mixed numbers and improper fractions; add and subtract fractions with different denominators.
- Decimals: Decimals up to 3 decimal places; addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division of decimals.
- Measurement: Compute area and perimeter of squares and rectangles; solve time problems involving 24-hour clocks.
- Geometry: Measure angles in degrees; identify line symmetry and symmetric patterns.
- Data: Complete, read, and interpret data presented in tables and line graphs.

### Year 5 (Primary 5)
- Whole Numbers: Numbers up to 10 million; order of operations (BODMAS/BIDMAS).
- Fractions: Multiply fractions by whole numbers, fractions, and mixed numbers; divide fractions by whole numbers.
- Decimals: Advanced decimal operations and multi-step word problems.
- Ratio: Concept of ratio; equivalent ratios; solve word problems comparing two or three quantities.
- Percentage: Concept of percentage; write fractions/decimals as percentages; find percentage of a quantity.
- Measurement: Calculate the area of a triangle; calculate the volume of cubes and cuboids in cubic centimetres/metres.
- Geometry: Properties of triangles (isosceles, equilateral, right-angled); angles on a straight line, angles at a point, and vertically opposite angles.
- Data: Calculate the average (mean) of a set of data.

### Year 6 (Primary 6)
- Algebra: Use algebraic expressions with one variable; simplify, evaluate, and solve basic linear algebraic equations.
- Fractions, Ratio, Percentage: Advanced 4-step word problems combining all three concepts.
- Speed: Concept of speed, average speed, distance, and time; solve multi-vehicle word problems.
- Measurement: Calculate the area and circumference of a circle; calculate area of composite 2D shapes; find volume of composite 3D solids.
- Geometry: Properties of quadrilaterals (parallelograms, rhombuses, trapeziums); find unknown angles using shape properties.
- Data: Interpret and extract information from pie charts.

## Secondary Level (Years 7 to 10)

### Year 7 (Secondary 1)
- Numbers: Primes, highest common factor (HCF), lowest common multiple (LCM), squares, cubes, real numbers, and the number line.
- Algebra: Algebraic manipulation, expansion, and simplification; solve linear equations and linear inequalities.
- Functions & Graphs: Cartesian coordinates; plot and interpret linear graphs (y = mx + c).
- Ratio, Proportion, Percentage: Direct and inverse proportion; percentage increase and decrease.
- Geometry: Basic angle properties (parallel lines, transversals); construction of triangles and angle bisectors.
- Statistics: Statistical data collection; read and construct histograms, stem-and-leaf diagrams, and scatter plots.

### Year 8 (Secondary 2)
- Algebra: Quadratic expansion and factorization; algebraic fractions and fractional linear equations.
- Functions & Graphs: Solve simultaneous linear equations using graphical, substitution, and elimination methods.
- Geometry: Pythagoras' theorem; volume and surface area of cylinders, cones, spheres, and pyramids.
- Trigonometry: Trigonometric ratios (sine, cosine, tangent) for acute angles in right-angled triangles.
- Probability: Sample space; probability of single events; experimental versus theoretical probability.
- Statistics: Measures of central tendency (calculate and compare mean, median, and mode).

### Year 9 (Secondary 3)
- Algebra: Solve quadratic equations by factorization, completing the square, and using the quadratic formula.
- Functions & Graphs: Plot quadratic, cubic, and reciprocal functions; solve equations graphically.
- Geometry: Properties of circles (angles in a segment, angles at centre, tangent properties); congruent and similar triangles.
- Trigonometry: Trigonometric ratios for obtuse angles; Area of a triangle (½ab sin C); Sine Rule and Cosine Rule.
- Mensuration: Arc length and sector area using radian measure.

### Year 10 (Secondary 4)
- Set Theory: Set notations, Venn diagrams, union, intersection, and complement of sets.
- Matrices: Matrix addition, subtraction, multiplication, and determinant of a 2×2 matrix.
- Vectors: Vector addition, subtraction, scalar multiplication, and position vectors in a 2D plane.
- Calculus (Introduction): Concepts of rates of change; basic differentiation of polynomials; equations of tangents.
- Statistics: Cumulative frequency curves; quartiles, percentiles, interquartile range, and standard deviation.

## Pre-University Level (Years 11 and 12)

Reflects the standard H2 Mathematics syllabus.

### Year 11 (Pre-U 1)
- Functions & Graphs: Graphing techniques, transformations of graphs, composite functions, and inverse functions.
- Equations & Inequalities: Systems of linear equations, polynomial equations, inequalities, and system modeling.
- Sequences & Series: Arithmetic and Geometric progressions; Sum to infinity; Sigma (Σ) notation.
- Vectors: Vector equations of lines and planes; scalar and vector products; intersection and distances between lines/planes.
- Complex Numbers: Cartesian form (a + bi) and Polar/Exponential form (re^(iθ)); De Moivre's theorem; solving polynomial equations with complex roots.

### Year 12 (Pre-U 2)
- Calculus (Differentiation): Derivatives of exponential, logarithmic, trigonometric, and inverse trigonometric functions; implicit differentiation; parametric differentiation.
- Calculus (Applications): Tangents and normals; rates of change; stationary points (maxima/minima); Maclaurin series expansion.
- Calculus (Integration): Integration techniques (substitution, by parts, partial fractions); definite integrals; area under a curve; volumes of revolution.
- Differential Equations: First-order differential equations; variables separable method; contextual application modeling.
- Probability & Statistics: Permutations and combinations; probability laws; Discrete Random Variables (Binomial and Poisson distributions); Continuous Random Variables (Normal distribution).
- Advanced Statistics: Sampling methods; Central Limit Theorem; Hypothesis testing (z-test and t-test); Linear regression and correlation coefficients.
