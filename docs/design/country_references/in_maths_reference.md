## Primary Level (Classes 1 to 5)

### Class 1
- Numbers: Count, read, write, and compare numbers from 1 to 99; concept of zero and place value (tens and ones).
- Operations: Simple addition and subtraction of single-digit and double-digit numbers without regrouping.
- Shapes & Space: Identify basic shapes (circle, triangle, square, rectangle); understand spatial terms (top, bottom, inside, outside).
- Measurement: Compare lengths, weights, and capacities using non-standard units (e.g., handspans, footsteps).
- Patterns & Time: Identify and extend simple patterns; sequence daily events and understand morning, afternoon, and night.

### Class 2
- Numbers: Numbers up to 999; place value up to hundreds; expanded form; greater than, less than, and equal to.
- Operations: Addition and subtraction with regrouping (carrying and borrowing); introduction to multiplication as repeated addition.
- Measurement: Introduction to standard units of length (metres, centimetres) and weight; tell time to the hour and half-hour; days of the week and months of the year.
- Geometry & Data: Identify straight and curved lines; identify basic 3D shapes (cone, cylinder, sphere); collect and record simple data tallying.

### Class 3
- Numbers & Money: Numbers up to 9,999; understanding Indian currency (Rupees and Paise) and converting money.
- Operations: Multi-digit addition and subtraction; multiplication tables up to 10; introductory division as equal sharing or grouping.
- Measurement: Add and subtract standard units of length, mass, and capacity; read analogue clocks to 5-minute intervals.
- Geometry & Patterns: Draw basic 2D shapes; explore tiling (tessellation) and symmetry; complete progressive numerical and shape patterns.

### Class 4
- Numbers: Large numbers up to 100,000; writing numbers in words and international/Indian numbering place-value charts.
- Operations: Multiplication of 3-digit numbers by 2-digit numbers; long division with remainders; multi-step arithmetic word problems.
- Fractions: Visualizing fractions; identifying numerator and denominator; equivalent fractions; fractional parts of a collection.
- Measurement & Geometry: Calculate perimeter of simple shapes; read calendars and calculate elapsed time; identify radius and diameter of a circle.
- Data: Represent data using pictographs and basic bar graphs.

### Class 5
- Numbers: Large numbers up to 10 million (crores); understanding factors, multiples, Prime, and Composite numbers.
- Operations: Highest Common Factor (HCF) and Lowest Common Multiple (LCM); operations involving large multi-digit figures.
- Fractions & Decimals: Addition, subtraction, and multiplication of fractions; introduction to decimals, place value of decimals, and basic decimal conversion.
- Measurement: Area and volume concepts; calculate the area of squares and rectangles; find the volume of a cube and cuboid.
- Geometry: Measure and draw angles using a protractor; identify types of angles (acute, right, obtuse); draw symmetrical reflections.

## Middle & Secondary Level (Classes 6 to 10)

### Class 6
- Number System: Knowing our numbers; whole numbers; playing with numbers (HCF/LCM applications); integers on a number line; fractions and decimals expansion.
- Algebra: Introduction to algebra using variables; formulating simple expressions and linear statements.
- Geometry: Basic geometrical ideas (points, lines, parallel lines, intersecting lines); understanding elementary 2D and 3D shapes.
- Mensuration: Detailed calculation of perimeter and area of rectilinear 2D patterns.
- Ratio & Proportion: Concept of ratio; unitary method for real-world scaling problems.
- Data Handling: Organization of data; pictographs and drawing scaled bar graphs.

### Class 7
- Number System: Properties of addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division of integers, fractions, and decimals.
- Algebra & Power: Solve single-variable linear equations; algebraic expressions (monomials, binomials, terms, coefficients); exponents and powers.
- Geometry: Lines and angles (pairs of angles, transversal configurations); properties of triangles (exterior angle property, angle sum property, triangle inequality).
- Congruence & Symmetry: Congruence of triangles (SSS, SAS, ASA, RHS criteria); rotational symmetry.
- Mensuration: Area of a parallelogram; area of triangles; circumference and area of a circle.
- Data Handling: Calculate arithmetic mean, median, and mode; probability of simple single chance events; double bar graphs.

### Class 8
- Numbers: Rational numbers and their properties; square roots and cube roots (prime factorization and long division methods).
- Algebra: Linear equations in one variable; algebraic expressions and identities; factorisation of algebraic identities.
- Geometry: Understanding quadrilaterals (convex, concave, parallelograms, rhombuses, trapeziums); practical geometry (constructing quadrilaterals).
- Mensuration: Surface area and volume of a cube, cuboid, and cylinder.
- Graphs & Data: Introduction to linear coordinate graphs; frequency distribution tables, histograms, and pie charts.

### Class 9
- Number Systems: Irrational numbers; real numbers and their decimal expansions; rationalizing the denominator; laws of exponents for real roots.
- Algebra: Polynomials (degree, zeros, Remainder Theorem, Factor Theorem); algebraic identities; linear equations in two variables.
- Coordinate Geometry: Cartesian plane; coordinates of a point; plotting coordinates across quadrants.
- Geometry: Euclid's geometry; lines and angles theorems; congruent triangles proofs; quadrilaterals properties; circles (chords, angles subtended, cyclic quadrilaterals).
- Mensuration: Heron's Formula for area of triangles; surface areas and volumes of spheres, hemispheres, cones, cylinders, and cuboids.
- Statistics: Graphical representation of data (histograms with varying base widths, frequency polygons).

### Class 10
- Board Examination Readiness — strict alignment with the CBSE Class 10 blueprint:
  - Number Systems: Real numbers; Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic; proving the irrationality of numbers (e.g., √2, √3).
  - Algebra: Zeros of a polynomial; relationship between coefficients and roots of quadratic equations; pair of linear equations in two variables; Arithmetic Progressions (AP — nth term and sum of n terms).
  - Coordinate Geometry: Distance formula; section formula (internal division).
  - Geometry: Similarity of triangles theorems (Thales/Basic Proportionality Theorem); circle tangents and properties.
  - Trigonometry: Trigonometric ratios of acute angles; trigonometric identities (sin²θ + cos²θ = 1); Heights and Distances (angles of elevation and depression).
  - Mensuration: Areas related to circles (segments and sectors); surface areas and volumes of combination solid models.
  - Statistics & Probability: Mean, median, and mode of grouped data; theoretical classical probability of events.

## Senior Secondary Level (Classes 11 and 12)

Splits into **Mathematics (041)** (science/engineering) or **Applied
Mathematics (241)** (commerce/social sciences). Below is 041 only.

### Class 11
- Sets & Functions: Set theory (unions, intersections, complements); relations and functions; trigonometric functions (domain, range, multi-angle formulas).
- Algebra: Principle of Mathematical Induction; complex numbers and quadratic equations; linear inequalities; permutations and combinations; Binomial Theorem; sequences and series (AP and GP frameworks).
- Coordinate Geometry: Straight lines (slope, various forms of line equations); conic sections (circles, ellipses, parabolas, hyperbolas); three-dimensional geometry introduction.
- Calculus: Limits and derivatives; intuitive understanding of continuity and rates of change.
- Statistics & Probability: Measures of dispersion (range, mean deviation, variance, standard deviation); axiomatic approach to probability.

### Class 12
- AISSCE Prep — the final nationwide evaluation matrix:
  - Relations & Functions: Types of relations (reflexive, symmetric, transitive, equivalence); inverse trigonometric functions.
  - Algebra: Matrices (types, operations, transpose, symmetric/skew-symmetric); determinants (inverse of a matrix, solving systems of linear equations using Cramer's rule/matrix inversion).
  - Calculus (Core Weightage): Continuity and differentiability; Rolle's and Lagrange's Mean Value Theorems; applications of derivatives (tangents, normals, increasing/decreasing functions, maxima/minima optimization).
  - Calculus (Integration): Definite and indefinite integrals (integration by parts, substitution, partial fractions); area under simple curves.
  - Differential Equations: Order and degree; general and particular solutions; homogeneous and linear differential equations of first order.
  - Vectors & 3D Geometry: Vector properties, dot product, cross product, scalar triple product; direction cosines and direction ratios of a line; shortest distance between two lines; equations of lines and planes in space.
  - Linear Programming: Mathematical formulation of LP problems; graphical solution method for two variables; feasible and infeasible regions.
  - Probability: Conditional probability; multiplication theorem on probability; Independent events; Bayes' Theorem; Random variables and their probability distributions.
