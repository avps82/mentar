MOE Singapore's science curriculum is Inquiry-Based, organised around five
cross-cutting themes rather than one linear topic list: **Diversity, Cycles,
Systems, Interactions, Energy**. Below, each bullet's theme/strand prefix is
kept exactly as supplied, since content-authoring will need it directly.

Science is formally introduced from Year 3 (Primary 3) — Years 1–2 carry no
separate science subject in the SG system (unlike AU, which now has Year 1
science). This is a genuine structural difference, not a gap: **do not add
SG Year 1/2 science pages** without checking this against the MOE syllabus
again — it's exactly the kind of thing the depth-program's benchmark rule
("verify, never assume") exists to catch.

## Primary Level (Years 3 to 6)

### Year 3 (Primary 3)
- Diversity (Living things): Characteristics of living vs. non-living things; classification of animals (mammals, birds, fish, insects, amphibians, reptiles).
- Diversity (Plants & Fungi): Classification of flowering vs. non-flowering plants; characteristics of fungi and bacteria.
- Diversity (Materials): Exploring properties of everyday materials (strength, flexibility, waterproof capabilities, ability to float/sink, transparency).
- Systems (Human): Basic overview of human body systems (skeletal, muscular, digestive, respiratory, circulatory).
- Systems (Plants): Parts of a plant (leaves, stem, roots) and their primary functions.

### Year 4 (Primary 4)
- Cycles (Life Cycles): Life cycles of plants; life cycles of various animals (3-stage cycles like grasshoppers vs. 4-stage cycles like butterflies and mosquitoes).
- Cycles (Matter): States of matter (solid, liquid, gas); properties of matter (mass, volume, shape stability).
- Energy (Light): Properties of light; shadows and factors affecting shadow sizes; reflection and absorption.
- Energy (Heat): Heat as a form of energy; heat flow from hotter to colder regions; conductors vs. insulators; temperature measurement and expansion/contraction.

### Year 5 (Primary 5)
- Cycles (Water): The water cycle; evaporation, condensation, melting, and freezing; importance of water conservation and water purity.
- Cycles (Plant & Human Reproduction): Processes of pollination, fertilization, and seed dispersal in plants; human reproductive system and heredity.
- Systems (Respiratory & Circulatory): In-depth study of human breathing, heart functions, blood vessels, and how they transport gases/nutrients.
- Systems (Plant Transport): How xylem and phloem transport water, minerals, and food throughout the plant.
- Systems (Electrical): Simple circuit configurations; open vs. closed circuits; conductors vs. insulators of electricity; batteries in series and parallel.

### Year 6 (Primary 6)
- Energy (Forms & Conversions): Kinetic, potential (gravitational, chemical, elastic), electrical, light, sound, and heat energy; tracking energy conversions in systems.
- Energy (Photosynthesis): The sun as the primary energy source; how plants make food using sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide.
- Interactions (Forces): Types of forces (gravitational, elastic spring, magnetic, frictional); effects of forces on speed, direction, and shape.
- Interactions (Environment): Ecology basics; populations, communities, and habitats; food chains and food webs.
- Interactions (Adaptations): Structural and behavioral adaptations of organisms for survival (breathing, coping with temperature extremes, defense, food collection).
- Interactions (Human Impact): Deforestation, global warming, pollution, conservation efforts, and sustainability.

## Secondary Level (Years 7 to 10)

Years 7–8 = Lower Secondary Science, one COMBINED curriculum (Physics +
Chemistry + Biology together — matches Mentar's existing junior-science
combined-subject shape). Years 9–10 branch into Pure or Combined Sciences
(Physics/Chemistry/Biology) for O-Level/N-Level — matches Mentar's existing
senior-science SPLIT-subject shape, one stage earlier than AU's Year 11 split.

### Year 7 (Secondary 1) — combined
- Scientific Method: Laboratory safety, apparatus usage, measurement of physical quantities, and experimental variables.
- Matter: Kinetic particle theory; physical/chemical changes; separation techniques (filtration, evaporation, distillation, chromatography).
- Cells: Structure of plant and animal cells; cell specialization; tissue, organ, and organ system hierarchy.
- Forces: Balanced and unbalanced forces; pressure, friction, and gravitational fields.
- Energy: Forms of energy; sources of energy; thermal energy transfer (conduction, convection, radiation).

### Year 8 (Secondary 2) — combined
- Atomic Structure: Atoms, elements, compounds, molecules, and the introduction to the Periodic Table.
- Chemical Interactions: Chemical reactions (combustion, thermal decomposition, oxidation); testing for acids and alkalis (pH scale).
- Human Systems (Advanced): Detailed analysis of the human digestive, circulatory, and reproductive systems; health and nutrition.
- Ecosystems: Nutrient cycles (carbon and nitrogen cycles); energy flow in ecosystems; conservation of biodiversity.

### Year 9 (Secondary 3) — split Physics / Chemistry / Biology
- Physics: Kinematics (speed, velocity, acceleration); dynamics (Newton's Laws of Motion); mass, weight, and density; turning effects of forces (moments); work, energy, and power.
- Chemistry: Atomic structure, chemical bonding (ionic, covalent, metallic); chemical equations and stoichiometry (The Mole Concept); acids, bases, and salts.
- Biology: Cell structure and organization; movement of substances (diffusion, osmosis); nutrients, enzymes, and plant nutrition/photosynthesis; human transport and gas exchange.

### Year 10 (Secondary 4) — split Physics / Chemistry / Biology
- Physics: Waves (light, sound, electromagnetic spectrum); electricity (current, static, circuits, practical electricity); magnetism and electromagnetism.
- Chemistry: The Periodic Table; metals (reactivity series and extraction); qualitative analysis (testing for ions and gases); atmosphere and environment; organic chemistry (alkanes, alkenes, alcohols, carboxylic acids).
- Biology: Coordination and response (nervous system and eye); hormones and homeostasis; cell division (mitosis and meiosis); heredity and molecular genetics (DNA); ecology and human impact on ecosystems.

## Pre-University Level (Years 11 and 12)

Reflects the H2 Science syllabi (students specialise at H1/H2/H3 depending on
goals — H2 is the reference level given here, same posture as the maths H2
reference already saved).

### Year 11 (Pre-U 1)
- Physics: Newtonian mechanics (vectors, kinematics, dynamics, forces, work, energy, power); circular motion; gravitational fields; oscillations; thermal physics.
- Chemistry: Atomic structure; chemical bonding; gaseous state; chemical energetics (thermodynamics); reaction kinetics; chemical equilibria (including acid-base equilibria).
- Biology: Cellular structure; biomolecules (carbohydrates, lipids, proteins); nucleic acids and gene expression (transcription, translation); stem cells; enzymes.

### Year 12 (Pre-U 2)
- Physics: Wave motion and superposition; electricity and magnetism (electric fields, current, DC circuits, electromagnetism, electromagnetic induction); quantum physics; nuclear physics.
- Chemistry: Electrochemistry; the Periodic Table (Period 3 periodicity, Group 2, Group 17, and Transition Elements); organic chemistry synthesis (hydrocarbons, halogen derivatives, hydroxy compounds, carbonyls, carboxylic acids, nitrogen derivatives).
- Biology: Cellular respiration and photosynthesis; cell signaling and communication; genetics and inheritance (Mendelian and molecular); evolution (mechanisms, natural selection); infectious diseases and immunology.

No Earth & Environmental Science course is named at any SG level in this
reference — unlike AU, which has one as a distinct senior subject. Recorded,
not assumed either way; confirm before deciding SG lacks the subject or it
was simply omitted from this list.
