NCERT-guided. Classes 1-2 have no dedicated science subject (folded into
language/maths); Classes 3-5 teach it as Environmental Studies (EVS,
"Looking Around" textbook) alongside social studies — a genuinely combined
subject, not separable into a pure-science strand set the way AU's Year 1-2

Biology integrated). Senior secondary (Classes 11-12) splits into medical
(PCB) or non-medical (PCM) elective tracks — the reference below covers both
P, C and B, since a student takes either PCB or PCM (both share Physics and
Chemistry; Biology is medical-only, Maths would be non-medical-only and is
already covered by in_maths_reference.md).

## Primary Level (Classes 1 to 5)

Classes 1-2: **no dedicated science subject** — do not add IN Class 1/2
science pages; this is a structural absence like SG's science starting at
Year 3, not a gap to fill.

Classes 3-5 taught as Environmental Studies (EVS), combining science and
social studies:

### Class 3 (EVS)
- Family & Friends: Relationships, work, play, animals (creatures that crawl, fly, or walk), and plants around us.
- Food & Water: Basic nutrition, sources of food, cooking methods, and the crucial need for clean drinking water.
- Shelter & Travel: Different types of houses, animal shelters, and exploring local modes of transportation.
- Sensory Awareness: Understanding the five sense organs and how humans and animals perceive their surroundings.

### Class 4 (EVS)
- Ecosystems & Habitats: Looking closely at root systems, types of trees, and how animals live together in groups or herds.
- Water Resources: The water cycle, floating vs. sinking objects, and identifying clean vs. polluted water sources.
- Food Production: Understanding the journey of food from farms to markets, and basic digestive functions.
- Waste Management: Introductory concepts of keeping the community clean and recycling basic materials.

### Class 5 (EVS)
- Super Senses: Advanced look at animal senses (e.g., how dogs scent or eagles spot prey) and animal behavior.
- Natural Resources: Conservation of forests, fuels (petroleum and coal), and understanding how seeds germinate and disperse.
- Health & Disease: Water-borne diseases (like malaria and cholera), structural hygiene, deficiency diseases, and basic first aid.
- Earth & Space: Historical perspectives on space travel (e.g., Sunita Williams) and looking at maps and basic landforms.

## Middle & Secondary Level (Classes 6 to 10)

Unified NCERT Science textbooks; Physics/Chemistry/Biology integrated, not
separate subjects, through Class 10.

### Class 6
- Food & Nutrition: Components of food (carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals) and balanced diets.
- Materials: Sorting materials into groups based on properties; separation of mixtures (winnowing, sedimentation, filtration).
- Living World: Characteristics of living organisms; body movements (bones, cartilage, joints); habitats and adaptations.
- Physics Essentials: Motion and measurement of distances; light, shadows, and reflections; basic electrical circuits and switches; properties of magnets.

### Class 7
- Nutrition & Life: Nutrition in plants (photosynthesis) and nutrition in animals (human digestive system); cellular respiration.
- Chemistry: Physical and chemical changes; introduction to acids, bases, and salts.
- Weather & Climate: Animal adaptations to extreme climates; understanding winds, storms, and cyclones.
- Physics & Nature: Heat and temperature measurement; light (reflection, spherical mirrors, lenses); electric current and its heating/magnetic effects.

### Class 8
- Microorganisms & Cells: Friends and foes (bacteria, fungi, viruses); structure and functions of plant and animal cells.
- Chemicals & Materials: Synthetic fibres and plastics; chemical properties of metals and non-metals.
- Production & Conservation: Crop production and management; conservation of plants and animals (biodiversity).
- Physics Concepts: Force and pressure; friction (factors affecting it); sound (amplitude, frequency, pitch); chemical effects of electric current; natural phenomena (lightning, earthquakes).

### Class 9
- Matter: Matter in our surroundings; is matter around us pure?; atoms and molecules; structure of the atom.
- Biology (Foundations): The fundamental unit of life (cell); plant and animal tissues; improvement in food resources.
- Physics (Mechanics): Motion (distance, displacement, velocity, acceleration); force and laws of motion; gravitation (universal law, mass vs. weight); work and energy; sound waves.

### Class 10
- Board Examination Readiness — strict alignment with the national assessment blueprint:
  - Chemical Substances: Chemical reactions and equations; acids, bases, and salts; metals and non-metals; carbon and its compounds (functional groups, homologous series).
  - World of Living: Life processes (nutrition, respiration, circulation, excretion); control and coordination (nervous system, hormones); how do organisms reproduce?; heredity (Mendelian genetics).
  - Natural Phenomena (Physics): Light reflection and refraction (lens formulas, power); human eye and the colorful world (dispersion, atmospheric refraction).
  - Effects of Current: Electricity (Ohm's Law, resistance in series/parallel, heating effects); magnetic effects of electric current (field lines, electromagnetic induction).
  - Environment: Ecosystems, food chains, and ozone layer depletion.

## Senior Secondary Level (Classes 11 and 12)

Splits into elective streams: **medical (PCB)** or **non-medical (PCM)**.
Physics and Chemistry are common to both; Biology is medical-only (PCB);
Mathematics is non-medical-only (PCM, already in in_maths_reference.md).

### Class 11
- Physics: Physical world and measurement; kinematics; laws of motion; work, energy, and power; motion of systems of particles and rigid body; gravitation; properties of bulk matter; thermodynamics; behavior of perfect gases and kinetic theory; oscillations and waves.
- Chemistry: Basic concepts of chemistry; structure of atom; classification of elements and periodicity; chemical bonding and molecular structure; thermodynamics; equilibrium (chemical and ionic); redox reactions; organic chemistry principles and techniques; hydrocarbons.
- Biology: Diversity of living organisms; structural organization in plants and animals; cell structure and function (cell division, biomolecules); plant physiology (photosynthesis, respiration, growth); human physiology (digestion, breathing, circulation, excretion, locomotion, neural/chemical control).

### Class 12
- AISSCE / Board Prep:
  - Physics: Electrostatics; current electricity; magnetic effects of current and magnetism; electromagnetic induction and alternating currents; electromagnetic waves; optics (ray and wave optics); dual nature of radiation and matter; atoms and nuclei; electronic devices (semiconductors).
  - Chemistry: Solutions; electrochemistry; chemical kinetics; d- and f-block elements; coordination compounds; haloalkanes and haloarenes; alcohols, phenols, and ethers; aldehydes, ketones, and carboxylic acids; amines; biomolecules (proteins, carbohydrates, nucleic acids).
  - Biology: Sexual reproduction in flowering plants; human reproduction and reproductive health; principles of inheritance and variation; molecular basis of inheritance; evolution; human health and diseases; microbes in human welfare; biotechnology principles, processes, and applications; organisms and populations; ecosystems; biodiversity and its conservation.
