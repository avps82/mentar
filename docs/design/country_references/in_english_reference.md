CBSE (NCERT-guided) structures English around three core sections across
every stage: Reading, Writing/Grammar, Literature. Primary uses the
Marigold/Mridang textbook series; Middle/Secondary uses Honeysuckle,
Honeycomb, Beehive, First Flight; Senior Secondary uses Hornbill, Snapshots,
Flamingo, Vistas.

---

## Primary Level (Classes 1 to 5)

### Class 1
- Reading & Phonics: Recognize letters; decode simple words; read short, illustrated poems and stories.
- Grammar Foundations: Nouns (naming words), pronouns (I, you, he, she), and basic prepositions (in, on, under).
- Writing: Trace letters; write simple words; complete short sentences with blanks.
- Speaking & Listening: Recite rhymes; follow simple 2-step instructions; introduce oneself clearly.

### Class 2
- Reading: Read simple paragraphs aloud with appropriate pronunciation; identify main characters in a story.
- Grammar: Singular/plural nouns, action words (verbs), describing words (adjectives), and simple present/past tenses.
- Writing: Write short sentences about familiar topics (family, pets); use capital letters and full stops.
- Speaking: Respond to simple questions; describe objects or pictures in a few words.

### Class 3
- Reading: Comprehend short unseen passages; identify the moral or central theme of a story.
- Grammar: Conjunctions (and, but, because), adverbs, gender nouns, and basic punctuation (commas, question marks).
- Writing: Paragraph writing (50-60 words); write short informal messages or leave-taking notes.
- Speaking & Listening: Narrate short experiences; participate in simple role-plays and class dialogues.

### Class 4
- Reading Comprehension: Answer direct and inferential questions from unseen texts and poems.
- Grammar: Pronouns (possessive and demonstrative), past continuous tense, articles (a, an, the), and antonyms/synonyms.
- Writing: Guided composition; informal letter writing to friends or family; picture composition.
- Speaking: Deliver short speeches on given topics; narrate stories with expressions.

### Class 5
- Reading: Analyze short stories and informational texts; locate specific details to answer questions.
- Grammar: Subject-verb agreement, types of sentences (assertive, interrogative, imperative, exclamatory), and modal auxiliaries (can, may).
- Writing: Essay writing (100 words); formal applications (e.g., leave requests to the school principal); story writing using hints.
- Speaking & Listening: Participate in group discussions; listen to audio tracks and extract key information.

## Middle & Secondary Level (Classes 6 to 10)

### Class 6
- Reading: Unseen factual and discursive passages; note-making basics.
- Grammar: Tenses (all forms), active and passive voice, and determiners.
- Writing: Notice writing, message writing, and descriptive paragraphs (person, place, or event).
- Literature: Understand themes, character traits, and basic poetic devices (rhyme scheme, similes) in prescribed chapters.

### Class 7
- Reading: Complex unseen passages; vocabulary-in-context testing.
- Grammar: Reported speech (direct/indirect), modal verbs, and prepositional phrases.
- Writing: Story writing from outlines, informal letters, and diary entries.
- Literature: Analyze character motives; explain stanzas of poetry in own words; identify metaphors.

### Class 8
- Reading: Discursive and literary passages requiring high-level interpretation.
- Grammar: Non-finites (infinitives, gerunds, participles), clauses (noun, relative, adverb), and editing/omission exercises.
- Writing: Formal letters (letters to the editor, business enquiries) and short articles (100-120 words).
- Literature: Critical appreciation of poems; answer long, value-based questions based on text themes.

### Class 9
- Reading: Two unseen passages (one discursive, one case-based factual with data/charts).
- Grammar: Tenses, modals, subject-verb concord, and reported speech (commands, statements, questions).
- Writing: Descriptive paragraphs (person/diary entry) and story writing based on a given cue/situation.
- Literature (Beehive & Moments): Detailed textual analysis; extrapolate themes; identify personification, alliteration, and irony.

### Class 10
- Board Exam Readiness — strict adherence to the CBSE Class 10 Core English blueprint:
  - Section A (Reading): Advanced inference, text evaluation, and vocabulary extraction across complex unseen texts.
  - Section B (Writing & Grammar): Formal Letters (complaints, ordering goods, letters to the editor) and Analytical Paragraphs (interpreting graphs, maps, or charts); integrated grammar gap-filling and transformation.
  - Section C (Literature — First Flight & Footprints Without Feet): Extract-based analytical questions, short answers, and long theme-comparison essays.

## Senior Secondary Level (Classes 11 and 12)

English Core: advanced academic, business, and vocational literacy; mature
literary texts (Hornbill, Snapshots, Flamingo, Vistas).

### Class 11
- Reading Skills: Unseen passages for comprehension; mandatory Note-Making and Summarizing sections using abbreviations.
- Creative Writing Skills: Short writing tasks (posters, advertisements) and long writing tasks (speech writing, debate writing).
- Grammar: Reordering of sentences, transformation of sentences, and error correction.
- Literature: Exploration of complex themes (identity, generation gaps, colonialism, nature) in prose and poetry; textual analysis of literary subtext.

### Class 12
- AISSCE (All-India Senior School Certificate Examination) Prep — the final national evaluation matrix:
  - Reading (Section A): High-level conceptual understanding, decoding, and analysis of unseen prose.
  - Creative Writing (Section B): Short tasks (Formal/Informal invitations and replies, Notices) and Long tasks (Letters to the editor/job applications with a detailed resume, and Article/Report writing).
  - Literature (Section C): Objective and subjective testing of prescribed literary masterpieces; evaluation of complex poetic devices, subtext, character evolution, and global thematic connections.

No course-split at Classes 11-12 (unlike AU's Essential/Mainstream/Literature
or SG's GP/KI substitution) — CBSE English Core is one continuous subject
through senior secondary. Do not invent a split here.
