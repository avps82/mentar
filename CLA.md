# Mentar Individual Contributor License Agreement (v1.0)

Thank you for contributing to Mentar. This agreement keeps the project's copyright
consolidated so its dual-licensing model (AGPL-3.0-only public licence + commercial
licences from the maintainer) stays possible. The plain-language reasoning — including
exactly what you give up and what you keep — is in [`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md).
Read that first; nothing here should surprise you after it.

## How to agree

Post this comment on your first pull request, before it is merged:

> I have read and agree to the Mentar Individual Contributor License Agreement
> (CLA.md, v1.0). — *Your full name*

The PR thread is the record of agreement. It covers that contribution and all your
future contributions to Mentar. If a future CLA version changes the terms, you will
be asked to agree again — a version bump never applies to you retroactively.

## Definitions

- **"You"** — the individual submitting the Contribution (or the legal entity on
  whose behalf it is submitted).
- **"Contribution"** — any original work of authorship you intentionally submit to
  Mentar for inclusion (code, documentation, curriculum content, test fixtures).
- **"Maintainer"** — the Mentar copyright holder identified in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright licence to use,
reproduce, modify, distribute, publicly display and perform, sublicense, and
**relicense your Contribution under any terms**, including commercial licences.

That last grant is the point of this document. In exchange, the Maintainer promises:
**every accepted Contribution is always also released under the AGPL-3.0-only** — the
open version can never be taken away from you or the community, whatever the
Maintainer does commercially.

You grant the Maintainer and all recipients of Mentar a perpetual, worldwide,

alleging your Contribution infringes their patent, any patent licences granted to
that party under this agreement terminate.

## 3. Your representations

You represent that:

- Each Contribution is your original creation, or you otherwise have the right to
  grant the licences above.
- If your employer has rights to intellectual property you create, you have their
  permission to submit the Contribution, or your employer has waived such rights.
- If a Contribution includes third-party material (including AI-generated content
  you did not direct and review yourself), you identify it and its licence in the
  pull request rather than passing it off as your own work.
- You will disclose anything you become aware of that would make these
  representations inaccurate.

## 4. No warranty, no support obligation

You provide your Contribution "AS IS", without warranties of any kind, and you are
not obliged to provide support for it. Mentar is a children's-safety-critical
project; the Maintainer reviews every Contribution and remains responsible for what
ships, but that review does not transfer your representations in §3.
