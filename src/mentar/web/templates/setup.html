Option C — Cloud AI (uses the internet, your account)

Read this before choosing a cloud option. With a cloud AI, your child's typed lesson answers and the tutor's replies are sent over the internet to the provider, every turn, under your account. You — not Mentar — become the data operator for that flow, and the provider's own data-handling terms apply. Mentar's usual "everything stays on this computer" promise does not fully apply while a cloud option is on. To turn it off later, come back here and pick a local option.
