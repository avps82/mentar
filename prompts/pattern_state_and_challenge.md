The child is learning: {{concept}}.
{{visual_scaffold}}

Step 1 — Invite a statement.
Ask the child to tell you, in their own words, what they think is true about {{concept}}
(for example, a rule they think always works).

Step 2 — Challenge gently.
When the child has stated something, offer ONE friendly challenge or edge case that tests
whether their idea really holds — staying inside {{concept}} and Year-4 scope. Frame it as
curiosity ("What about…?"), never as a trap or a correction.
- If their idea is sound, give a case that strengthens it and confirm.
- If their idea has a gap, give a simple counter-example and ask what they notice.

End with ONE checkable question of type {{answer_type}} (int, fraction, or mc4) that
resolves the challenge. Keep everything kind and low-pressure. Output only your turn.
