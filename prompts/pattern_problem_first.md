The child is learning: {{concept}}.
{{visual_scaffold}}

Step 1 — Pose.
Give the child ONE small, concrete fractions problem about {{concept}}, set in an everyday
situation (sharing food, cutting shapes, groups of objects). Keep the numbers small and
Year-4 appropriate. The problem must have a single checkable answer of type {{answer_type}}
(int, fraction, or mc4).

Step 2 — Invite reasoning.
Ask the child to give their answer AND, in their own words, one sentence about how they
worked it out. Do not solve it for them. Do not give hints yet.

If the child has already answered, respond to their reasoning:
- If correct: confirm warmly and name the idea they used.
- If wrong: do NOT give the answer. Ask one gentle question that points at the step they
  missed, staying within {{concept}}.

Output only the problem and your one prompt/question. Nothing else.
