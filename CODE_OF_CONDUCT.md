# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This project builds software for **children**. That raises the bar, not lowers it: content,
examples, and discussion in issues, PRs, and docs must be appropriate for that context.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning
  from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community and the children this software serves

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery of any kind, and sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without
  their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities and scope

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take
appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour deemed inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful. This Code applies within all project spaces (issues,
PRs, discussions, docs) and when an individual is officially representing the project.

## Reporting

Report violations privately via **GitHub → Security → "Report a vulnerability"** on this
repository (the same private channel used for safety reports — it reaches the maintainer
directly), or via a direct message to the maintainer on GitHub. All reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
