When explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Idiom check — literal vs. figurative meaning** (use for idiom-recognition questions):
```
Phrase                          Literal meaning            Figurative (idiom) meaning
"break a leg"                   injure your leg            good luck
"it's raining cats and dogs"    animals falling from sky   raining very heavily
"spill the beans"               drop actual beans          reveal a secret
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give the phrase and ask what it really means, or which option is an idiom.
- Always show the literal reading beside the real one -- the gap IS the idiom.
