When explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Active vs. passive voice — who does the action?** (use for voice questions):
```
ACTIVE:   [SUBJECT does the action]      "The dog chased the cat."
PASSIVE:  [SUBJECT receives the action]  "The cat was chased by the dog."
                                                              ↑
                                            the DOER moves to the end (or disappears)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Ask who is DOING the action; passive puts the receiver first and the doer last (or hides it).
- Neither voice is wrong -- passive is a choice about what to foreground.
