**Cohesive devices — what the join tells the reader**:
```

CAUSE AND EFFECT  therefore, consequently, as a result, because of this
SEQUENCE          firstly, meanwhile, subsequently, finally
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Ask what RELATIONSHIP the connective signals, not just to pick one that fits.
- The join carries meaning: swap "however" for "therefore" and the argument changes.
