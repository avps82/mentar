When explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Syntax for effect — sentence length is a choice**:
```
SHORT sentence     "It failed."                     -> impact, finality
LONG sentence      "The room, still smelling of rain  -> builds detail,
                    and old paper, filled slowly       slows the reader down
                    with people who had waited
                    outside for hours."
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give a short and a long sentence; ask what effect each creates.
- Neither is better; the question is what the writer wanted the reader to feel.
