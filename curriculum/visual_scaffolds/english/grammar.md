When writing or explaining a grammar question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Label-the-word layout** — annotate each word's role above or below it:
```
The  quick  fox   jumps  gracefully.
 ↑     ↑     ↑      ↑        ↑
art  adj.  noun   verb      adv.
```

**Sentence-slot frame** — blank slots for the target word class:
```
The _______  cat sat on the _______ mat.
   (adjective)              (adjective)
```

**Punctuation markup** — show where marks are missing or wrong:
```
the dog ran quickly but the cat sat still
^                  ^
capital?           comma before "but"?
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Year 2–3: noun/verb/adjective only; label-the-word or sentence-slot frame.
- Year 4–5: adverbs, pronouns, conjunctions; introduce comma and apostrophe rules.
- Year 6+: clauses, phrases, complex sentences; full punctuation scope.
- Show the sentence first; then ask the targeted question. Never ask about more than one grammar rule per question.
