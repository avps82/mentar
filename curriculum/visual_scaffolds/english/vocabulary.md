When writing or explaining a vocabulary question, use the Frayer model structure:

**Frayer model (four-quadrant layout)**:
```
+----------------------+----------------------+
|     DEFINITION       |  CHARACTERISTICS     |
|  (in own words)      |  (key features)      |
+--------[WORD]--------+----------------------+
|      EXAMPLES        |   NON-EXAMPLES       |
|  (things it IS)      |  (things it is NOT)  |
+----------------------+----------------------+
```

For a multiple-choice question, anchor ONE quadrant as the stem and test the student's ability to complete another.

**For phonics / decoding (Year 2–3)**:
- Highlight the target phoneme or grapheme within the word (e.g. bold the digraph "sh" in "ship").
- Provide a rhyming pair as context ("ship rhymes with ___ : chip / chop / shop / stop").

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Year 2–3: definition + one example; single syllable or CVC words.
- Year 4–6: all four quadrants active; multi-syllable words, prefixes/suffixes.
- Year 7+: include etymology or root word in the CHARACTERISTICS quadrant.
- Describe the quadrant structure in words for the child-facing question; do not draw a raw grid.
