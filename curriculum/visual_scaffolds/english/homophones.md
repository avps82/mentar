When explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Same sound, different job**:
```
SOUNDS THE SAME        MEANS                        USE IT IN
there                  in that place                "over there"
their                  belonging to them            "their bags"
they're                they are                     "they're late"

to                     in the direction of          "to school"
too                    also / more than enough      "me too", "too hot"
two                    the number 2                 "two cats"

here                   in this place                "come here"
hear                   to listen                    "I can hear you"
```

**Swap test** — put the meaning in and see if the sentence still works:
```
"Put it over ___."   ->  in that place  ->  there
"___ bags are wet."  ->  belonging to them -> their
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give the MEANING and ask for the word, never the word and ask for the meaning of a word you printed.
- Spelling is the whole point here: the sound cannot tell them apart.
