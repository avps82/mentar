When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
SOLILOQUY: alone on stage ──► audience hears the mind
DRAMATIC IRONY: we know ✔, they know ✘ — the gap is the tension
ASIDE: one line flicked to us mid-scene, unheard on stage
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Mark who can hear what — the technique IS the hearing diagram.
