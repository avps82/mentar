When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
BANDWAGON: “join two million Australians…”  (belonging)
AUTHORITY: “9 out of 10 dentists…”          (borrowed trust)
EMOTION:   the shivering puppy               (feelings first)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Ask what the ad wants you to FEEL before what it wants you to buy.
