When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
FACT: checkable — “the vote was 7–2”
OPINION: a judgement — “a disgraceful decision”
PERSPECTIVE: who was asked, what they're called, what's left out
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Run the check-test: could evidence prove this sentence wrong?
