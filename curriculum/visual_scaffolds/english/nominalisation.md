When explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Verb or adjective  ->  noun**:
```
VERB / ADJECTIVE        NOMINALISATION (the noun form)
decide                  decision
arrive                  arrival
fail                    failure
happy                   happiness
assess                  assessment
```

**Common endings that signal it**:
```
-tion   -sion   -ment   -ance   -ence   -ness   -al   -ure
decision  assessment  arrival  happiness  failure
```

**Why formal writing does it** — the action becomes a thing you can discuss:
```
plain:      "They decided quickly, and the school failed to explain it."
nominalised:"The decision was quick, and the failure to explain it..."
             (denser, more formal -- and it hides WHO acted)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give a mix of plain verbs/adjectives and their noun forms; ask which is nominalised.
- Worth naming the trade-off: nominalisation sounds formal but can hide the actor.
