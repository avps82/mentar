When explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**One of many, or the name of ONE?**
```
COMMON NOUN (no capital)     PROPER NOUN (capital letter)
city                         London
river                        the Nile
country                      Australia
day                          Monday
girl                         Priya
```

**Test**: could there be lots of them?
```
"a city"      -> yes, many cities        -> common noun
"London"      -> only one with that name -> proper noun, capital L
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Ask which word needs a capital, and why -- the capital is the visible consequence.
- Days, months, people and places are the everyday proper nouns.
