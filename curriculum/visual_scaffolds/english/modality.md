When explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Modality dial — how certain the wording makes a claim**:
```
LOW  <───────────────────────────────────────────>  HIGH
might  could  perhaps  possibly | often | always  must  never  certainly
(leaves room for doubt)                    (leaves no room at all)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give a word or short claim and ask how certain it sounds.
- High modality is not "better" — over-claiming is a weakness in an argument.
