When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
same text, three lenses:
feminist      → who speaks, who is spoken for?
Marxist       → who owns, who labours?
postcolonial  → whose land, whose story?
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- A lens is a question you bring — state the question, then read.
