When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
RIGHT THERE: the text says it — point at the line
CLUES: text hints + your thinking = inference
OPINION: yours — fine, but not evidence
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Ask which of the three the question wants before answering.
