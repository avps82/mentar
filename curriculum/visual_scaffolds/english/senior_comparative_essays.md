When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
BLOCK:            POINT-BY-POINT:
  A A A             idea 1: A│B
  B B B             idea 2: A│B
  (compare at end)  (compare in every paragraph)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- The linking sentence — “where A does X, B does Y” — is the skill to show.
