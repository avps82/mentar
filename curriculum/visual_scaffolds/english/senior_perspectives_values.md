When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
WHO is speaking? ──┬── participant (“I was there”)
                   ├── expert (trained judgement)
                   └── interested party (follows the money)
then ask: what do they VALUE, and what does that hide?
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Attach every claim to a speaker before judging it.
