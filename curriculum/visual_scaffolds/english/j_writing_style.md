When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
TELL: “She was nervous.”
SHOW: “Her hands trembled as she reached for the letter.”
VARY: long flowing sentence, then a short sharp one.
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Put show and tell side by side — the difference teaches itself.
