When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
power rule: derivative of axⁿ = n × a × xⁿ⁻¹
bring the power down as a multiplier, then lower the power by one
3x²  →  2×3x = 6x        5x³  →  3×5x² = 15x²
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Two moves only: multiply by the old power, subtract one from it.
