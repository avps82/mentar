```key
values:   10  12  14  9  13
window:  [10  12  14]        → (10+12+14) ÷ 3 = 12
slide:        [12  14  9]    → next average
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Draw the bracket around the window; the average belongs to its middle month.
