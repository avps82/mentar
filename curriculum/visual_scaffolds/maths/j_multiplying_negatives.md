When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
   multiply the digits, then decide the sign

   (+) x (+) = (+)        same signs      -> positive
   (-) x (-) = (+)
   (+) x (-) = (-)        different signs -> negative
   (-) x (+) = (-)

   the same rule works for dividing
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Work the size first, sign second: 3 x 4 = 12, then decide + or -.
- Split out of `negative_numbers.md` on 2026-08-22, which served its THERMOMETER
  here. A thermometer shows where a directed number SITS and is the right picture
  for adding and subtracting; it says nothing about why two negatives multiply to
  a positive. That file kept the sign rule as one line of prose and never drew it.
- Do not explain the rule as "two minuses cancel" -- it is about direction
  reversing twice, and the cancelling story breaks the moment they meet
  subtraction of a negative.
