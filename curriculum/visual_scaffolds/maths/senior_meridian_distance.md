When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
1° of latitude ≈ 111 km (given in the question)
distance = degrees apart × 111
5° → 5 × 111 = 555 km
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Both places must share a line of longitude — say so in the question.
