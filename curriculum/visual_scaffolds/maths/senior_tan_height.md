When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
tan(angle) = opposite ÷ adjacent
height = distance along the ground × tan(angle)
   |‸ height (opposite)
   |__θ____  distance (adjacent)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Label opposite and adjacent relative to the marked angle, not to the page.
