When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
F = m × a     (kg × m/s² = N, newtons)
5 kg at 3 m/s² → F = 15 N
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Always finish with the unit line: kilograms times m/s² makes newtons.
