When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
prediction: substitute x into  y = a + b×x
b = slope (change in y per 1 of x), a = intercept (y when x = 0)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Mark the substituted x on the horizontal axis and read up to the line, then across.
