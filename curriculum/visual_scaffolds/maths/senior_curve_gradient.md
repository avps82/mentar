When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
1. differentiate    2. substitute the x-value
y = x² + 4x → dy/dx = 2x + 4 → at x = 3: 2×3 + 4 = 10
the answer is the slope of the tangent line touching the curve there
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Differentiate FIRST; substituting into y instead of dy/dx is the classic slip.
