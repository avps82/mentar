When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
standard error = population standard deviation ÷ √n
σ = 12, n = 9 → SE = 12 ÷ √9 = 12 ÷ 3 = 4
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Take √n as its own numbered step before dividing.
