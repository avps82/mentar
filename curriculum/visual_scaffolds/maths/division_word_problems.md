When writing or explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Sharing (partitive division)** — distribute objects equally into groups:
```
12 apples shared between 3 children:
[●●●●] [●●●●] [●●●●]
child 1  child 2  child 3   → 4 each
```

**Grouping (quotitive division)** — count how many equal groups fit:
```
12 ÷ 4 = ?   "How many groups of 4 in 12?"
[●●●●] [●●●●] [●●●●]
  1       2       3   → 3 groups
```

**Array (rows × columns)**:
```
12 ÷ 4:
● ● ● ●
● ● ● ●
● ● ● ●   → 3 rows of 4
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Word problems: always say what IS being shared/grouped and how many groups (not just abstract numbers).
- Sharing context: "X things shared equally among Y people — how many each?"
- Grouping context: "X things packed in groups of Y — how many groups?"
- If remainder is in scope: state it explicitly ("with R left over").
- Keep divisors ≤ 10 for Year 2–4; up to 12 for Year 5+.
