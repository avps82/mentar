When writing or explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Labelled rectangle** — always draw and label sides:
```
  +──────────────+
  │              │  ↑
  │              │  4 cm
  │              │  ↓
  +──────────────+
       6 cm
```

**Perimeter** — the distance all the way around (add all sides):
```
Perimeter = 6 + 4 + 6 + 4 = 20 cm
          = 2 × (length + width)
          = 2 × (6 + 4) = 20 cm
```

**Area** — the space inside (length × width):
```
Area = 6 × 4 = 24 cm²

Visualise as a grid of unit squares:
□□□□□□
□□□□□□
□□□□□□
□□□□□□   ← 6 × 4 = 24 unit squares
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Always draw the shape and label dimensions; never give numbers without context.
- Distinguish perimeter (length of boundary, linear units: cm, m) vs. area (space inside, square units: cm², m²).
- Year 6: rectangles and squares only. Year 7+: compound shapes (split into rectangles).
- Give units in the question; expect units in the answer.
