When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
E = sum of (value × its probability)
value        1     2     3     6
probability 1/4   1/4   1/4   1/4   →  E = (1+2+3+6) ÷ 4 = 3
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Lay values and probabilities out as a table before multiplying anything.
