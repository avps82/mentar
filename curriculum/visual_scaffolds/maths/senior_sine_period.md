When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
period of sin(kx°) = 360 ÷ k
sin(x°):  ∿∿ one full wave every 360°
sin(2x°): ∿∿∿∿ twice as many waves — period 180°
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- A bigger k squeezes more waves into the same width; the period shrinks.
