When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
under = below     behind = at the back
between = in the middle of two
🌳  🐈  (the cat is BEHIND the tree if the tree hides it)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- One position word per question, acted out by two everyday things.
