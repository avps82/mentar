When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
same 20 m of fence, different rectangles:
1×9 → 9 m²    2×8 → 16 m²    5×5 → 25 m²  ← the square wins
side of the best square = perimeter ÷ 4
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Show at least two worse rectangles first — the square being best is the discovery.
