When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
paid $10, cost $7:
change = 10 − 7 = 3 → $3
count UP from the cost: 7 → 8 → 9 → 10 (three jumps)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Show both ways: subtraction and counting up — shops count up.
