When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
missing cell = its row total − the known cells in that row
         yes   no  | total
  girls   12    ?  |  20     ? = 20 − 12 = 8
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Check the answer against the COLUMN total too — both must agree.
