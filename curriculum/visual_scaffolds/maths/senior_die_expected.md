When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
fair die numbered 1 to n:  expected value = (n + 1) ÷ 2
1 2 3 4 5 6 7 → values pair off around the middle: E = 8 ÷ 2 = 4
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Show the pairing (1 with n, 2 with n−1, …) — that is WHY the middle is the mean.
