When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
new value = old value × growth factor, applied once per year
growth factor = 1 + rate ÷ 100
start ──×factor──▶ after year 1 ──×factor──▶ after year 2
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Show each year as one arrow; never jump straight to a power for Essential/General learners.
