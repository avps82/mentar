When writing or explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Number line with jumps** — show skip counting as equal hops:
```
Count by 3s:
0    3    6    9   12   15
|----|----|----|----|----|-→
 +3   +3   +3   +3   +3
```

**Pattern table** — position and value side by side:
```
Position:  1    2    3    4    5
Value:     3    6    9   12   15
Rule: × 3 (or +3 each time)
```

**Doubles / halves visual** — fold or split:
```
Double 7:   |●●●●●●●|●●●●●●●|  = 14
Half of 14: |●●●●●●●|           = 7
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Always show at least 3 terms of the pattern before asking for the next.
- State the rule explicitly in the explanation (not in the child-facing question).
- Skip counting: say what to count by ("count by 4s starting from 0").
- Doubles up to 20; halves of even numbers up to 40 (Year 2–3).
- Year 4+: extend to 3-digit numbers; introduce term-to-term and position-to-term rules.
