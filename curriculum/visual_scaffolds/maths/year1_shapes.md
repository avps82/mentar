When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
△ triangle: 3 sides    □ square:   4 sides
  pentagon:  5 sides      hexagon:  6 sides
trace the edge — count each straight side once
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Number each side as it is counted (1, 2, 3…).
- Pentagon and hexagon are NAMED, not drawn. Their glyphs (⬠ U+2B20, ⬡ U+2B21)
  were measured in chromium at 1.37 monospace cells against this stylesheet's
  --font-mono, so every row below them slid sideways -- and on a machine without
  even a proportional fallback they are tofu boxes. △ U+25B3, □ U+25A1 and
  ○ U+25CB all measure exactly 1.00 and are safe. DESIGN.md forbids downloaded
  fonts, so there is no fallback to reach for: a named shape a child can read
  beats a drawn one that breaks the table (2026-08-22).
