When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
claim: “every X has property P”
counterexample: ONE X without P — and the claim is dead
“all primes are odd” → 2 is prime, 2 is even → disproved
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Check both halves: the candidate must satisfy the conditions AND break the conclusion.
