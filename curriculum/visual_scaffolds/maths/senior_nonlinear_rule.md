When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
y = x² + c: square first, then add
x:  1   2   3   4
y:  1+c 4+c 9+c 16+c   — the jumps GROW: that is what non-linear means
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Contrast with a straight line's equal jumps in one sentence.
