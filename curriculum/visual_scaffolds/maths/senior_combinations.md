When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
combinations = ordered count ÷ r!   (order does not matter)
teams of 2 from 5: ordered 5×4 = 20, each team counted 2! = 2 ways → 20 ÷ 2 = 10
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Divide out the repeats: every group was counted once per ordering of itself.
