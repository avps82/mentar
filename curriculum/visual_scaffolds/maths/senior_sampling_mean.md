When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
mean of the sampling distribution = the population mean (unchanged)
population: ────────●────────  mean μ
sample means:    ──▂▅█▅▂──     centred on the SAME μ, just narrower
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Sample size changes the spread, never the centre — draw both curves on one axis.
