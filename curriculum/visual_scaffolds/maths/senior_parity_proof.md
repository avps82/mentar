When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
even = 2k        odd = 2k + 1
odd + odd = (2a+1) + (2b+1) = 2(a+b+1) → even
odd × odd = (2a+1)(2b+1) = 2(2ab+a+b) + 1 → odd
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Always convert to 2k form first; the conclusion reads off the final shape.
