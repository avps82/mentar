When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
pay = normal hours × rate  +  overtime hours × (rate × overtime multiplier)
e.g. time-and-a-half → overtime rate = normal rate × 1.5
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Split the pay into the two blocks first; add them only at the end.
