When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
fuel needed = distance ÷ 100 × rate (L per 100 km)
300 km at 8 L/100 km → 300 ÷ 100 = 3 → 3 × 8 = 24 L
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Work out how many 100-km blocks the trip is first; the rate applies per block.
