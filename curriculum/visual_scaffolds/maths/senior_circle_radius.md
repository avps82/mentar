When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
x² + y² = r²  is the circle of radius r centred at the origin
x² + y² = 25 → r² = 25 → r = √25 = 5
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- The equation shows r SQUARED — taking the square root is the whole task.
