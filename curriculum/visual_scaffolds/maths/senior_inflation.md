When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
new price = old price × (1 + rate ÷ 100)
equivalently: new price = old price + old price × rate ÷ 100
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Keep to ONE year of inflation; repeated years belong to the repeated-growth scaffold.
