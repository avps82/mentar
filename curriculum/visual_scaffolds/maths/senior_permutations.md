When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
ordered choices multiply: n × (n−1) × (n−2) × …  (r factors)
3 prizes to 3 of 5 people: 5 × 4 × 3 = 60
slot 1   slot 2   slot 3
  5    ×   4    ×   3
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Draw one slot per position; each slot has one fewer choice than the last.
