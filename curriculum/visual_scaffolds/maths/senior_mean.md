When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
mean = sum of the values ÷ how many values
4, 7, 7, 2 → total 20 → 20 ÷ 4 = 5
levelling: ▂▅▅▁ → ▃▃▃▃  (same total, made equal)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Always state the total and the count separately before dividing.
