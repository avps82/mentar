When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
the Earth turns 360° in 24 h → 15° every hour
hours apart = degrees of longitude apart ÷ 15
60° apart → 60 ÷ 15 = 4 hours
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- East of you is LATER in the day; keep direction out of it unless asked.
