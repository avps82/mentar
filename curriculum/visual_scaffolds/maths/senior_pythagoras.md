When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
c² = a² + b²   (c = hypotenuse, opposite the right angle)
      /|
  c  / | b     the square on the longest side equals
    /__|       the other two squares added
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Mark the right angle and label the hypotenuse before any squaring.
