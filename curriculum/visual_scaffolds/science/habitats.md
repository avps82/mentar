When writing or explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Water vs. land** — side-by-side comparison:
```
LIVES MAINLY IN WATER           LIVES MAINLY ON LAND
  fish, dolphin, crab, octopus    lion, spider, snail, rabbit
  (breathes/moves through water   (breathes air, moves on ground
   as its main home)               as its main home)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give ONE animal and ask whether it lives mainly IN water or ON land — "mainly" matters,
  since some animals (a frog, a crab) use both; keep examples to animals with one clear
  main habitat, not amphibious edge cases.
- A habitat is WHERE a living thing lives, not a stage of its life cycle (that's a
  different concept — see `life_cycles.md`) and not what class of animal it is (mammal/
  fish/bird — see `living_nonliving.md`).
