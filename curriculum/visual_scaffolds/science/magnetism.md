When writing or explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Attracted vs. not attracted** — side-by-side comparison:
```
ATTRACTED TO A MAGNET               NOT ATTRACTED TO A MAGNET
  an iron nail, a steel spoon       a wooden pencil, a plastic ruler
  a steel paperclip, a steel screw  a rubber band, a glass marble
  (contains iron or steel)          (wood, plastic, rubber, glass —
                                      no iron or steel inside)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give ONE object and ask whether a magnet would attract it or not.
- The key test: does the object contain iron or steel? Most metals (aluminium, copper,
  gold) are NOT attracted to an everyday magnet — don't imply "metal = magnetic" as a
  shortcut, since that's the most common misconception at this level.
- A magnet acts at a short distance too, not just on contact — but keep the question to
  simple "is it attracted, yes or no" recognition, not distance effects.
