When explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**A group is a column, and the column is the outer-electron count**:
```
GROUP 1   alkali metals   1 outer electron    lithium, sodium, potassium
          -> lose it easily, very reactive

GROUP 17  halogens        7 outer electrons   fluorine, chlorine, bromine
          -> one short of full, grab one, very reactive

GROUP 18  noble gases     full outer shell    helium, neon, argon
          -> nothing to gain or lose, almost unreactive
```

**Reactivity follows the shell, not the element**:
```
easy to LOSE one electron   -> Group 1 reactive
easy to GAIN one electron   -> Group 17 reactive
nothing to do               -> Group 18 unreactive
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Name an element and ask its group, or a group and ask for its behaviour.
- Reactivity is always explained by the outer shell.
