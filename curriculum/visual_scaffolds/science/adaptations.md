When writing or explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Adaptation vs. not linked to survival** — side-by-side comparison:
```
BODY FEATURE (helps it survive)     NOT A BODY FEATURE (man-made, not part of it)
  a polar bear's thick fur            a dog's collar
  a camel's hump                      a horse's saddle
  a duck's webbed feet                a bird's cage
  (grown as PART of the animal,       (something a HUMAN adds —
   shapes how it survives)             not part of the animal itself)
```

**Feature → why it helps**:
```
FEATURE                →  WHY IT HELPS SURVIVAL
thick fur (polar bear) →  keeps the animal warm in the cold
webbed feet (duck)     →  helps it swim/paddle in water
long neck (giraffe)    →  reaches leaves high in trees
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give ONE item and ask whether it IS a body-feature adaptation, or is not (a man-made
  object associated with an animal, not part of its body).
- Keep to features with an obvious, well-known survival link — don't invent obscure ones.
