When writing or explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Is it a planet?** — side-by-side comparison:
```
IS A PLANET                     IS NOT A PLANET
  Earth                           the Sun (it's a star)
  Mars                            the Moon (it goes around Earth)
  Jupiter                         a comet
  Saturn                          a star
  Venus
```

**Simple order-from-the-Sun line** (for "what goes around what"):
```
SUN  →  MERCURY  →  VENUS  →  EARTH  →  MARS  →  ...
        (planets go AROUND the Sun; the Sun does not go around anything)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Multiple-choice only (verifier uses curated fact tables, never LLM assertion).
- Keep it to ONE simple fact per question: "is this a planet or not" — not the planets'
  order, distances, or sizes (out of scope at this level).
- The Sun is a STAR, not a planet — this is the single most common mix-up; if the
  question offers the Sun as an option, treat it clearly as "not a planet".
- The Moon goes AROUND Earth (a moon/satellite), it is not a planet itself.
