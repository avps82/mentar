When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
stratosphere — ozone layer, absorbs UV, calm
troposphere  — bottom ~12 km: ALL weather, us
greenhouse: sunlight in → surface warms → gases trap outgoing heat
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Natural greenhouse ≠ enhanced greenhouse — keep the two words apart.
