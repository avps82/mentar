When explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Three ways across the membrane**:
```
DIFFUSION         high -> low concentration     no energy   oxygen into blood
                  (any particle, down the gradient)

OSMOSIS           water only, across a          no energy   water into a root
                  partially permeable membrane              hair cell

ACTIVE TRANSPORT  low -> HIGH concentration     needs ATP   minerals into a root
                  (uphill, against the gradient)            from dilute soil water
```

**One question sorts them**:
```
Is it water through a membrane?        -> OSMOSIS
Is it going UP the gradient?           -> ACTIVE TRANSPORT
Otherwise, down the gradient           -> DIFFUSION
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give a real transport example and ask which process it is.
- Uphill always costs energy -- that is the whole distinction.
