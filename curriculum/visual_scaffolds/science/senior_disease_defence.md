When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
infectious: pathogen invades (flu, TB)      — can spread
non-infectious: no pathogen (diabetes, scurvy) — cannot
defence: white cells engulf → antibodies lock onto ONE pathogen
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Classify by asking one question: is there an invader?
