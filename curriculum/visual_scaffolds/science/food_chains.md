When writing or explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Producer vs. consumer** — side-by-side comparison:
```
PRODUCER (makes its own food)     CONSUMER (eats other living things)
  a tree, grass, a sunflower        a lion, a rabbit, a shark
  (uses sunlight to make food —     (gets its energy by eating
   this is called photosynthesis)    a producer or another consumer)
```

**Simple food chain** (energy flows one way, arrow means "is eaten by"):
```
SUNLIGHT  →  GRASS (producer)  →  RABBIT (consumer)  →  FOX (consumer)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give ONE living thing and ask whether it IS a producer or a consumer.
- Only plants (and some other things that use sunlight, like seaweed/algae) are producers
  at this level — every animal is a consumer, no exceptions to introduce here.
- Don't ask about decomposers or specific consumer sub-types (herbivore/carnivore/
  omnivore) unless the node explicitly covers that distinction.
