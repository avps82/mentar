When explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**What survives a collision**:
```
ALWAYS conserved        total momentum          (in every collision)
                        total energy            (counting heat and sound)

NOT conserved in an     total KINETIC energy    (some becomes heat, sound,
inelastic collision                              and bent metal)
                        each object's own speed
```

**Before and after**:
```
before:  ->o  o        two objects, total momentum p
after:      oo  ->     stuck together, still total momentum p
                       but less kinetic energy than before
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Ask what is conserved, with kinetic energy as the deliberate near-miss.
- "Energy is conserved" is always true; "kinetic energy is conserved" is not.
