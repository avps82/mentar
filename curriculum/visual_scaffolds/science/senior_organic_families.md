When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
alkane   C−C   single bonds only      …-ane   methane, ethane
alkene   C=C   one double bond        …-ene   ethene, propene
alcohol  −OH   hydroxyl group         …-ol    ethanol
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- The name ENDING carries the family — point at the suffix explicitly.
