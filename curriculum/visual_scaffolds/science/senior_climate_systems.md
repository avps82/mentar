When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
ocean currents: equator ──warmth──► poles (the climate flywheel)
carbon cycle: air ⇄ forests ⇄ soil/sea ⇄ burning
past climate: ice-core bubbles · tree rings (nature's record books)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Proxies answer 'how do we KNOW?' — pair every claim with its evidence.
