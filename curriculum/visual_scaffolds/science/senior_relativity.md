When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
postulate: light speed c is the SAME for every observer
moving clock:   tick……tock   (runs slow as seen from the ground)
moving object:  ◄──┤ shorter along its motion
everyday proof: GPS clocks need correcting
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Always name WHO is observing — every relativity statement is about a viewpoint.
