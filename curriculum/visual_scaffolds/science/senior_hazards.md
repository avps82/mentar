When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
earthquakes + volcanoes: mostly AT plate boundaries
  plates → strain builds → sudden release (magnitude scale is logarithmic)
cyclones: born over WARM tropical ocean, spin round a calm eye
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Location is the clue: plate edges for quakes, warm seas for cyclones.
