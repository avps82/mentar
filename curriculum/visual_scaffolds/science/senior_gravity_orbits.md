When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
field arrows point AT the mass; strength fades with distance²
W = m × g   (weight is a force; mass never changes)
orbit = falling forever:  ──►
                          ↓ ⤵  sideways fast enough to keep missing
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- The cannonball picture: faster and faster sideways throws until one orbits.
