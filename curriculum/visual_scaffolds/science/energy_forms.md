When explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Three stores, by what puts energy in**:
```
GRAVITATIONAL POTENTIAL   lifted up high        a book above a desk, water behind a dam
KINETIC                   moving                a rolling ball, a moving car
ELASTIC POTENTIAL         stretched or squashed a rubber band, a compressed spring
```

**Follow the energy as it transfers**:
```
raised ball   --release-->   falling ball   --lands-->   heat + sound
gravitational                kinetic                     dissipated
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give an everyday object and ask which store dominates.
- Energy is never used up: name where it goes next.
