When writing or explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Renewable vs. non-renewable** — side-by-side comparison:
```
RENEWABLE (naturally replaced,     NON-RENEWABLE (takes millions of
never runs out)                     years to form, will run out)
  solar power, wind power             coal, oil
  hydroelectric power, biomass        natural gas, nuclear fuel (uranium)
```

**Quick test**:
```
Will this source naturally replenish itself within a human lifetime?
  YES  →  renewable (sunlight, wind, moving water, growing plants)
  NO   →  non-renewable (formed over millions of years, limited supply)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give ONE energy source and ask whether it is renewable or non-renewable.
- Coal, oil and natural gas are "fossil fuels" — all non-renewable, formed from ancient
  organic matter over millions of years.
