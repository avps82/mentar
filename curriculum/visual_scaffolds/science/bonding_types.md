When explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**What the atoms do with their electrons**:
```key
IONIC       metal + non-metal      electrons TRANSFERRED    NaCl, MgO
            Na -> Na+ ... Cl-      opposite charges attract

COVALENT    non-metal + non-metal  electrons SHARED         H2O, CO2, CH4
            H:O:H                  shared pairs

METALLIC    metal only             a SEA of free electrons  Cu, Fe, Al
            + + + + +              positive ions in it
             e- e- e-
```

**Tell them apart by behaviour**:
```
conducts when molten or dissolved, high melting point   -> IONIC
low melting point, often a gas or liquid                -> COVALENT (simple)
conducts as a solid, bendable, shiny                    -> METALLIC
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give a substance and ask which bonding it has; the metal/non-metal split is the clue.
- Say WHY the property follows from the structure.
