When explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**The spectrum, in order**:
```
LOWER ENERGY  <-------------------- visible light --------------------> HIGHER ENERGY
longer wavelength                                            shorter wavelength

radio   microwave   infrared   | VISIBLE |   ultraviolet   X-rays   gamma rays
```

**Which side of visible light?**
```
infrared, microwaves, radio   -> LOWER energy than light we can see
ultraviolet, X-rays, gamma    -> HIGHER energy than light we can see
```

**Everyday use, in the same order**:
```
radio broadcasts · phone signals · heat lamps · | seeing | · sunburn · medical images · cancer treatment
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Ask which side of visible light a radiation sits on, or to order two of them.
- All of them travel at the same speed in a vacuum; only wavelength and energy differ.
