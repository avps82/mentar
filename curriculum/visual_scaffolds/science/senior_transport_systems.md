When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
plant:  xylem ↑ water (roots → leaves)   phloem ⇅ sugars
animal: heart → arteries → capillaries → veins → heart
lungs:  O₂ in across alveoli, CO₂ out
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- One organism per question — plant or animal, never both in one stem.
