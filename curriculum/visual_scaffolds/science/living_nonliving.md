When writing or explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**MRS GREN — 7 life processes** (tick if the thing does ALL of these):
```key
M — Movement
R — Respiration (releasing energy from food)
S — Sensitivity (responding to the environment)
G — Growth
R — Reproduction
E — Excretion (removing waste)
N — Nutrition (taking in food/energy)

Living things do ALL 7. Non-living things do NOT.
```

**Two-column sort**:
```
Living           | Non-living
-----------------|----------------
dog, tree, daisy | rock, car, water
(do MRS GREN)    | (do NOT do all 7)
```

**Animal group classification table**:
```
Group    | Key features                | Examples
---------|-----------------------------|-----------------
Mammal   | warm-blooded, fur, live young| dog, whale, bat
Bird     | warm-blooded, feathers, beak | eagle, penguin
Fish     | cold-blooded, scales, gills  | salmon, shark
Insect   | 6 legs, 3 body parts        | bee, ant, beetle
Reptile  | cold-blooded, dry scales    | snake, lizard
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Living vs. non-living: give ONE example and ask if it is living/non-living AND why (MRS GREN).
- Animal groups: give ONE animal's key features and ask which group it belongs to.
- Watch the common misconceptions: a virus (not alive by MRS GREN), a bat (mammal, not bird), a whale (mammal, not fish).
- Multiple-choice only for verification; ground truth is from curated fact tables.
