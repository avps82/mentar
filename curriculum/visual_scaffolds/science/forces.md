When writing or explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Contact vs. non-contact force** — side-by-side comparison:
```
CONTACT FORCE (needs touching)       NON-CONTACT FORCE (acts at a distance)
  pushing a door, kicking a ball       gravity pulling an apple down
  friction between shoes and ground    magnetism pulling a paperclip
  pulling a rope                       static electricity attracting hair
  (the two things must be touching     (the force works even with a GAP
   for the force to act)                between the two things)
```

**Quick test**:
```
Are the two objects touching when the force acts?
  YES  →  contact force
  NO   →  non-contact force (gravity, magnetism, static electricity)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give ONE example and ask whether it is a contact or non-contact force.
- The 3 non-contact forces at this level are gravity, magnetism and static electricity —
  every other everyday force (push, pull, friction) is a contact force.
