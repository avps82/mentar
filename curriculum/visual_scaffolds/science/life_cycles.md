When writing or explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Life-cycle chain** (stages happen in order, then repeat):
```
EGG  →  YOUNG FORM  →  ADULT FORM  →  (lays eggs / has young, cycle repeats)

Butterfly:  egg → caterpillar → chrysalis → adult butterfly
Frog:       egg → tadpole → froglet → adult frog
Plant:      seed → seedling → mature plant → flowers/seeds
Chicken:    egg → chick → adult hen/rooster
```

**Stage vs. not-a-stage** — side-by-side comparison:
```
LIFE-CYCLE STAGE                NOT A LIFE-CYCLE STAGE
  egg, caterpillar, tadpole       a rock, a car, a chair
  seedling, chick                 (non-living, or a body part —
  (a form the living thing IS)     not a form the thing passes through)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give ONE item and ask whether it IS a life-cycle stage of a living thing, or is not.
- Keep stage examples to widely-known cycles (butterfly, frog, plant, chicken) — don't
  invent unfamiliar species.
- A life-cycle stage is a FORM the living thing itself passes through, not a place it
  lives (that's a habitat, a different concept) or a body part.
