When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
renewable: sun, wind, managed timber  — replenished on OUR timescale
non-renewable: coal, gas, ores        — millions of years to form
sustainable use: recycle metals · fishing quotas · use less
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- The test is the clock: does it come back within a human lifetime?
