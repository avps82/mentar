```key
air:   burning fuels → CO₂ up      CFCs → ozone thinning
water: fertiliser runoff → algal blooms   plastics → oceans
land:  clearing → habitat loss (the biggest extinction driver)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- One sphere per question — air, water or land.
