When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
   ┌───────────┐ crust  — thin, solid, us
   │  mantle   │ hot rock, flows slowly, moves the plates
   │  ┌─────┐  │
   │  │core │  │ iron: liquid outer, solid inner → magnetic field
   └──┴─────┴──┘
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Always give the scale feeling: the crust is the eggshell.
