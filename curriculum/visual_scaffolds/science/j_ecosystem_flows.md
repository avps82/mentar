When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
ENERGY: sun → plant → animal → heat (ONE WAY, ~90% lost each step)
NUTRIENTS: soil → plant → animal → decomposers → soil (a LOOP)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Two arrows, two shapes: a one-way street and a roundabout.
