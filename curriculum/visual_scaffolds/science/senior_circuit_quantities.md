When writing or explaining this question, use this visual structure:

```key
V = I × R
voltage  = the PUSH (volts)      — voltmeter goes ACROSS
current  = the FLOW (amperes)    — ammeter goes IN the loop
resistance = the OPPOSITION (ohms)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Water-pipe analogy allowed: pump pressure / flow rate / pipe narrowness.
