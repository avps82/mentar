When writing or explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Conductor vs. insulator** — side-by-side comparison:
```
CONDUCTOR (lets electricity flow)   INSULATOR (blocks electricity)
  copper wire, a steel key            a rubber band, a plastic ruler
  aluminium foil, a silver spoon      a wooden spoon, a glass rod
  (metals — electricity flows          (rubber, plastic, wood, glass —
   through them easily)                 electricity cannot flow through)
```

**Simple circuit**:
```
BATTERY  →  WIRE  →  BULB  →  WIRE  →  back to BATTERY
(a conductor everywhere in the loop lets the bulb light up;
 one insulator anywhere in the loop breaks the circuit — bulb stays off)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give ONE material and ask whether it is a conductor or an insulator.
- Metals are conductors; rubber, plastic, wood and glass are insulators — this is the
  reliable rule at this level, no exceptions to introduce.
- Don't require circuit-diagram drawing/reading unless the node explicitly covers circuits
  as a whole, not just conductor/insulator material recognition.
