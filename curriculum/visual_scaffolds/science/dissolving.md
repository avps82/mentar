When writing or explaining this question, use ONE of these visual structures:

**Dissolves vs. does not dissolve** — side-by-side comparison:
```
DISSOLVES IN WATER              DOES NOT DISSOLVE IN WATER
  salt, sugar                     sand, small stones
  instant coffee powder           pepper, oil
  (spreads out and disappears     (stays visible, or floats/sinks,
   into the water — you can't      doesn't spread out and vanish)
   see separate grains anymore)
```

**What "dissolve" means**:
```
BEFORE: salt grains + water (grains visible)
   ↓ (stir)
AFTER:  salty water (grains gone, but the salt is still there --
         you'd see it again if the water evaporated)
```

**Guidelines for the question text:**
- Give ONE substance and ask whether it dissolves in water or not.
- Oil does NOT dissolve — it separates and floats, a common one to get right.
- Dissolving isn't disappearing forever: the substance is still there, just spread out too
  thin to see (don't need to explain evaporation/recovery unless the node asks about it).
